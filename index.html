About Me

Hello! I’m Shuai Liu, a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Neurobiology at the University of Maryland School of Medicine, where I work with the Lobo Lab.

I earned my PhD in Neuroscience from the Medical College of Wisconsin in 2024. My research investigates neural ensembles involved in drug seeking, reward, and related behaviors, with a focus on the medial prefrontal cortex and dopamine systems.

Research & Skills

My work combines behavioral neuroscience and circuit-level approaches to study substance use, reward, and stress-related behavior.

Methods: rodent surgery, drug self-administration, optogenetics, chemogenetics, immunohistochemistry, RNAscope, confocal imaging, and behavioral analysis.
